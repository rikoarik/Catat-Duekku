# PRD — Catat Duekku V1 with AI

## Tujuan

Membantu pengguna mencatat dan memahami keuangan pribadi melalui aplikasi atau Telegram, dengan AI sebagai asisten pencatatan.

## Platform

- Android
- iOS
- Web
- Telegram

## Fitur inti

### Authentication

- signup;
- login;
- logout;
- profile name;
- timezone.

### Dashboard

- current balance;
- monthly income;
- monthly expense;
- remaining debt;
- recent transactions;
- spending by category;
- concise AI insight;
- quick add.

### Transactions

- manual create;
- AI natural-language create;
- receipt/screenshot extraction;
- list;
- search;
- filter;
- detail;
- edit;
- soft delete;
- confirmation before AI result is saved.

### Accounts

- Tunai
- Bank
- E-Wallet

### Categories

Income:

- Saldo Awal
- Gaji
- Refund
- Lain-lain

Expense:

- Makan & Harian
- Kos
- Kuota
- Transport
- Belanja
- Kesehatan
- Hiburan
- Pendidikan
- Pembayaran Utang
- Lain-lain

### Debts

- create/edit/delete;
- total;
- paid;
- remaining;
- due date;
- status;
- payment;
- payment creates linked expense transaction;
- atomic database operation.

### Telegram

- account linking;
- explicit commands;
- natural-language input;
- receipt photo;
- preview and confirmation;
- duplicate update prevention.

### AI insight

AI answers simple questions using monthly aggregates and debt summary.

### Import/export

- spreadsheet preview;
- valid/invalid rows;
- duplicate prevention;
- CSV export;
- XLSX export.

## Not in V1

- bank sync;
- transfers;
- investments;
- billing;
- family wallet;
- live Google Sheets sync;
- autonomous financial actions;
- AI saving without confirmation.
